Wheel fitment FAQ

Does this calculator guarantee a wheel will fit?

No. It compares dimensions mathematically, but cannot verify brake clearance, suspension clearance, fender clearance, load rating, or vehicle-specific fitment. Always confirm with a qualified wheel/tire professional.

What does wheel offset mean?

Offset (ET) is the distance in millimeters between the wheel's mounting face and its centerline. A higher positive offset generally moves the wheel farther inward; a lower offset generally moves it outward.

Will a wider tire always stick out farther?

Not always. Wheel width, offset, tire model, and the rim's measured width all affect the final position. This tool estimates nominal rim position only; tire sidewall shape can vary.

Important Fitment & Safety Disclaimer

Use at your own risk. Results are approximate calculations, not a guarantee of fitment, safety, legality, or performance. Actual clearance depends on vehicle trim, wheel construction, tire make/model, alignment, load, steering angle, suspension travel, brake components, manufacturing tolerances, and modifications. Confirm bolt pattern, hub bore, load rating, hardware, brake clearance, and dynamic tire clearance with the vehicle and component manufacturers or a qualified professional. Never drive a vehicle that rubs, vibrates abnormally, or has uncertain wheel fastening. The vehicle owner is responsible for independently verifying compatibility before purchase and use.
